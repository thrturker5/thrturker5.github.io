Privacy Policy

Chuby Buby Automation · Effective 10 November 2026

Chuby Buby Automation ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Chuby & Buby YouTube channel to manage that channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

Only after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's channel details, video list and statistics;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports;
  • youtube.upload (only if the owner separately enables publishing): to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channel.

The app does not request access to Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google service, and it does not access data belonging to anyone other than the signed-in channel owner.

How the data is used

  • Channel and video statistics and analytics are stored in a local database on the owner's computer to track performance over time and produce a daily report for the owner.
  • Upload permission is used only to upload videos the owner has explicitly approved, as private videos with a scheduled publication time.
  • Optionally, at the owner's direction, the text of the daily report (aggregated channel statistics, with no personal data about viewers) may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to produce written suggestions for the owner. It is used only to provide those suggestions to the owner.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server and no cloud database.
  • Google OAuth tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so that only the owner's Windows account can read them.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone, except the optional AI report analysis described above. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data.

Children

The YouTube channel makes content for children, but the app itself is used only by the adult channel owner and collects no data from or about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube provides to channel owners.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Chuby & Buby YouTube channel.

Chuby Buby Automation is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
